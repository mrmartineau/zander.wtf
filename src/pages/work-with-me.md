## SEO, AEO and GEO

Three names, one job: make your site easy to find, easy to read and easy to trust.

- **SEO** (search engine optimisation) helps you rank in Google and Bing.
- **AEO** (answer engine optimisation) helps tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews quote your site as the answer.
- **GEO** (generative engine optimisation) is the same aim from the other side: being named and cited in AI-written answers.

The base is the same for all three: clean HTML, clear copy and structured data. I do the technical work in your code and the copy work on your pages.

What I do:

- **Audit.** I check titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags, sitemaps, `robots.txt` and page speed. You get a short list of fixes in priority order.
- **Structured data.** I add JSON-LD (`Person`, `Organization`, `WebSite`, `Article`, `FAQPage`) so machines know what each page is, who wrote it and when.
- **Copy.** I rewrite page titles and opening paragraphs so each page names what you do and answers one clear question near the top.
- **Crawler access.** I set up `robots.txt` rules for search and AI crawlers, and add feeds and an `llms.txt` file. Evidence says `llms.txt` does little today, so I treat it as a cheap extra, not a fix.
- **Check.** I test the result in Google's Rich Results Test and in the answer engines themselves.

This site is a working example. View source on any [blog post](/blog) to see the structured data. Read the [colophon](/colophon) for how it is built. Nobody can promise a rank or a place in an AI answer, and I won't promise one. **I can make sure nothing on your side stops you getting one.**
