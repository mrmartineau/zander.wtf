Something broken, confusing, or missing in one of my iOS apps? Email me at **[support@zander.wtf](mailto:support@zander.wtf)** and I'll get back to you.

There's no ticket system and no bot — it's just me reading the mail.

## What to include

The more of this you can tell me, the faster I can help:

- **Which app**, and its version — usually in the app's own settings or about screen
- **What you expected to happen**, and what happened instead
- **How to make it happen again**, if you can
- **Your device and iOS version** — **Settings → General → About**
- **A screenshot or screen recording**, if it's something you can see

If the app crashed, it helps enormously if you have crash reporting turned on in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers**. Those reports reach me automatically and are anonymous.

## Feature requests

Very welcome. Same address. Tell me what you're trying to do rather than how you think it should work — that usually leads somewhere better.

I can't promise anything gets built. I make these apps in my spare time, and I'd rather say nothing than promise a date I'll miss.

## Response times

I'm one person in the UK, so replies land on my schedule, not a support desk's. Most messages get an answer within a few days. If a week goes by with nothing, send it again — it'll have got lost, not ignored.

## Purchases, subscriptions and refunds

Anything involving money is handled by Apple, not me. Manage or cancel a subscription in **Settings → your name → Subscriptions**, and request a refund through [Apple's refund process](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204084). I can't issue refunds or see your payment details.

## Your data

I don't collect any. The [privacy policy](/privacy) explains what that means in practice, and the [terms & conditions](/terms) cover the rest.
