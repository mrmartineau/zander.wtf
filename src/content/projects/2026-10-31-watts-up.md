Check your MacBook's charger wattage, charging power, battery health and charge status from Raycast. Published on the Raycast store.

- **Show Power and Charger Info.** Lists the negotiated adapter wattage, voltage × current and charging status, plus charge level, power flow, health, cycle count and temperature.
- **Power Wattage in Menu Bar.** Shows the negotiated wattage, such as 85 W, when plugged in, or the charge level, such as 72%, on battery. It refreshes every minute and has more detail in the dropdown.
- **Show Energy Impact.** Lists the apps and processes that use the most energy, on Activity Monitor's "energy impact" scale.
- **Manage Energy Settings.** Shows the power source and time remaining, toggles Low Power Mode and Power Nap, and lists the sleep timers and the processes that block sleep.

Energy Impact takes one `top` sample of about 2 seconds when you open it. Nothing runs in the background. The Low Power Mode and Power Nap toggles call `pmset`, so macOS asks for your admin password.
