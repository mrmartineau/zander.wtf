[Watts Up](/projects/watts-up) is a new Raycast extension that shows what your MacBook's charger is doing. Show Power and Charger Info lists the negotiated adapter wattage, voltage × current and charging status. It also lists charge level, health, cycle count and temperature. Power Wattage in Menu Bar shows the wattage, such as 85 W, when you're plugged in, and the charge level, such as 72%, when you're on battery.

Two more commands cover the rest. Show Energy Impact lists the apps that use the most energy, on Activity Monitor's "energy impact" scale. Manage Energy Settings shows the power source and time remaining, and toggles Low Power Mode and Power Nap.

Energy Impact takes one `top` sample of about 2 seconds when you open it. The two toggles call `pmset`, so macOS asks for your admin password. It's on the [Raycast store](https://www.raycast.com/mrmartineau/watts-up) and the source is in the [raycast/extensions repo](https://github.com/raycast/extensions/tree/main/extensions/watts-up).
